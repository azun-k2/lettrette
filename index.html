What it does

Lettrette opens email that nothing else on your Mac will: winmail.dat attachments (TNEF) and .msg message files, on your Mac, iPhone and iPad. One purchase covers all three.

  • The whole message, as it was sent. From, to, cc, date and subject; the HTML, rich text or plain body with its inline pictures; messages attached to messages; and the raw headers when you need them.
  • Invitations and contact cards. Meeting invitations open with Add to Calendar, contact cards with Add to Contacts.
  • Get the attachments out. Preview them with Quick Look, drag them out, use Open With, or Save All at once.
  • Previews in Finder and Mail. Quick Look previews and Finder thumbnails for these files: press Space to see inside a message without opening it.
  • Share › Lettrette. A Share extension opens a file straight from Mail or another app.
  • Export. Save a message as .eml or as a PDF (A4 or US Letter), or export many messages or a whole folder at once, as EML, PDF or just their attachments.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later; on iPhone and iPad, iOS and iPadOS 17 or later.

Privacy in one paragraph

Lettrette never connects to the internet. Remote images in a message are never loaded, and nothing you open leaves your device. There is no account, no tracking and no analytics, and no data is collected. The full privacy policy says the same at more length.
