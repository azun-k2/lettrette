Download

All the files in one archive, with the batch export folder:

Download lettrette-samples.zip (0.8 MB)

Every person, address and message in these files is made up: the addresses are at example.com, example.org and example.net, which exist for examples only. The files were written by Lettrette's own test tools, not by a mail program, and contain no real mail.

How to open them

On the Mac

  • Choose File › Open… in Lettrette, or click Open… in its welcome window, and pick a file. You can also drop files on the welcome window.
  • Or select a file in Finder and press Space to see it with Quick Look (turn on Lettrette's Quick Look extension first, in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions › Quick Look).
  • For batch export, choose File › Batch Export…, add the batch-export folder, pick EML, PDF or Attachments, and click Export.

On iPhone and iPad

  • Tap a file below and save it to Files (or save the archive and tap it in Files to unzip it).
  • In Lettrette, tap Open… and pick the file; or tap the file in Files, then the Share button, and choose Lettrette.

The files

winmail.dat 299 KB
A TNEF attachment, the kind some mail programs send instead of a normal message. Shows:
  • an HTML body with an inline picture (the logo, a cid: image) and a table;
  • From, To, Cc and the date;
  • two attachments to preview with Quick Look and save: a 3-page PDF and a photo.
winmail-rtf.dat 2 KB
A TNEF message whose body is compressed RTF: bold, italic, a bulleted list, a heading in another font and colour.
message.msg 374 KB
An Outlook .msg file. Shows:
  • an HTML body with an inline picture;
  • a PDF and a photo as attachments;
  • an attached message (an embedded .msg, listed as “Paper samples for the spring catalogue.eml”): double-click it to open it in its own window, with its own photo attachment.
meeting-invite.msg 9 KB
A meeting invitation (IPM.Schedule.Meeting.Request): the invitation card with the time, place and attendees, Add to Calendar, and Export › Export as Calendar Event… (.ics).
contact.msg 8 KB
A contact (IPM.Contact): the contact card with job, phones, address and web page, Add to Contacts, and Export › Export as Contact Card… (.vcf).
batch-export/ 4 files, 329 KB
Four short messages, three .msg files and one winmail.dat, for File › Batch Export… on the Mac:
  • cover-photo.msg, with a photo;
  • proof-2.msg, with a PDF;
  • new-logo-winmail.dat, with a picture;
  • print-schedule.msg, text only.

Lettrette also has a sample message of its own: on the Mac, choose File › Open the Sample Message (or Open the Sample in the welcome window); on iPhone and iPad, tap Open the Sample.
