The short version

Lettrette collects no data. It never connects to the internet, so nothing you open can leave your device. There is no account, no tracking and no analytics.

Your messages stay on your device

Lettrette reads the files you open on your Mac, iPhone or iPad and shows them there. It never sends them anywhere, and it never loads remote images or other content a message points to on the internet.

Files you export (EML, PDF or attachments) are saved only where you choose.

On the Mac, the app carries Apple's "outgoing connections" permission only because the system's web engine, which draws message bodies, needs it to start. Lettrette blocks every web address a message points to, so nothing is ever fetched.

The Share extension

When you use Share › Lettrette, the file is copied into a temporary folder shared only between Lettrette and its own extension (an App Group) on your device, so the app can open it. That folder is private to Lettrette and nothing in it leaves your device.
